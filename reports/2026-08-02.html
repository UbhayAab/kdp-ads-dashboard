10. WHAT WE HAVE LEARNED, ACCUMULATED

  • Auto beats manual on this catalogue, twice measured, gap widening. 2.3x

cheaper per order on 2026-07-31, 3.6x today.

  • Loose match converts here despite the outside benchmark saying to kill it.
  • Amazon's SUMMARY report silently omits live entities. Always pull DAILY.
  • **A rollup written into a table keyed on (entity, day) corrupts quietly and

compounds.** Nothing errors; the totals just grow.

  • Overlap is only dangerous at the wrong grain. At daily grain, re-pulling a

settled day is idempotent. The 29-of-31-day overlap we were running was harmless

arithmetic and enormously wasteful effort.

  • A gap is permanent and an overlap is not. Amazon will not re-serve a window

past 31 days. That asymmetry is why the loop checks continuity every night.

  • A test that requires a freeze will lose it unless something is watching. The

cover test died to a change made the next day by a different script.

  • Royalty read off KDP beats royalty computed. The 50%/60% cliff at $9.99 and

real print costs decide which books can afford a delivering click, and no bid

change substitutes for that.
