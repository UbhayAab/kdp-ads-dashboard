Evidence · the constraint

Most of the account is deliberately below the delivery floor

Nothing on this account has ever won a real search impression below about $0.36. A sub-floor bid does not buy less traffic; it buys only the detail-page placements nobody competes for. After the 12 August tail-down, 2,704 enabled targets sit under that line on purpose.

The number that governs everything: $0.36 ÷ 8.08% = $4.46 of royalty needed before a floor bid is honest. Seven US books clear it and none of the seven has ever converted. The flagship, which is 16 of the account's 28 lifetime orders, carries $2.19.

This is why bids are not the lever. Raising them scales the loss; lowering them below $0.36 switches the keyword off. The gap closes with price and royalty, or it does not close.
