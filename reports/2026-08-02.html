10. WHAT WE HAVE LEARNED, ACCUMULATED

  • Auto beats manual on this catalogue, twice measured, gap widening. 2.3x

cheaper per order on 2026-07-31, 3.6x today.

  • Loose match converts here despite the outside benchmark saying to kill it.
  • Amazon's SUMMARY report silently omits live entities. Always pull DAILY.
  • **A rollup written into a table keyed on (entity, day) corrupts quietly and

compounds.** Nothing errors; the totals just grow.

  • Overlap is only dangerous at the wrong grain. At daily grain, re-pulling a

settled day is idempotent. The 29-of-31-day overlap we were running was harmless

arithmetic and enormously wasteful effort.

  • A gap is permanent and an overlap is not. Amazon will not re-serve a window

past 31 days. That asymmetry is why the loop checks continuity every night.

  • A test that requires a freeze will lose it unless something is watching. The

cover test died to a change made the next day by a different script.

  • Royalty read off KDP beats royalty computed. The 50%/60% cliff at $9.99 and

real print costs decide which books can afford a delivering click, and no bid

change substitutes for that.

  • Amazon's minimum daily budget differs by marketplace. AU refuses A$1.00 and

accepts A$1.50. Measured, not guessed, after it silently cost 15 campaigns.

Two things that had to be fixed first, and both were real

1. The budget would have blacked out the account. The tool defaulted to

$5.00/day. 179 campaigns at $5.00 is $895/day of new enabled budget on top of

$405/day already enabled, against a $344.83/month ceiling. The spend guard's

response to a projected breach is to pause *every campaign*, so an over-budgeted

expansion does not overspend, it stops the account partway through the month. Cut

to Amazon's floor before launching.

2. Amazon's floor is not the same everywhere. All 15 AU launches failed on the

first run with BUDGET_OUT_OF_MARKET_PLACE_RANGE. Probing directly: **A$1.00

rejected, A$1.50 accepted.** Now recorded per market in MARKET_MIN_BUDGET and

measured rather than assumed. AU relaunched, 15/15.

What this is actually a bet on, stated honestly

**Every bid in this expansion is below the ~$0.35 this account has been observed to

need to deliver.** Median affordable bid is $0.20-$0.24 across these markets. So

the honest hypothesis is not "auto will make money abroad", it is:

Do these marketplaces have any demand reachable at a price these books can
pay? Seven months of manual campaigns and 176 dead targets never answered that,
because they only ever tested keywords we guessed in English.

If they take zero impressions, that is the answer and it points at price, not

campaigns. Cost of finding out: 179 campaigns at the floor, on an account measured

to spend 1.5-3% of enabled budget. Check registered for 2026-08-08.

What was deliberately NOT done

Harvesting converting auto terms into exact match. It is the obvious next step

and the account says it is wrong: US EXACT holds **1,016 live keywords producing

157 impressions and zero clicks**, while BROAD produced 57 of the account's clicks

from 427 targets. Promoting a winner into exact moves it into the only part of the

estate with no evidence of working. Written into the tool so it is not re-proposed.
